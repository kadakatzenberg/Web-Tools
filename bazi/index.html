Chinese metaphysics holds that a moment in time has a composition — a mix of the five phases, changing continuously as the earth moves around the sun. BaZi (八字, eight characters) is the notation for it: four pillars of two characters each, taken from the year, month, day and double-hour of a birth.

05 Ten Gods · 十神  /  Five Structures

Five relationships to the Day Master.

Take the Day Master as self. Every other character stands in one of five relations to it — and each relation splits in two by polarity, giving the Ten Gods. Joey Yap's system names the five as Structures and the ten as Profiles.

How the Structure is read. Classical practice takes it from the Month Branch — the 月令, the seat of seasonal command — by asking what the principal hidden stem of that branch is to the Day Master. That is the method used by the instrument below.

07 Coda · 說

命由天定
運由己造 Heaven fixes the chart. What you do with it is not in the chart.

A BaZi chart is a description of the qi you were issued, not a verdict on what you will do with it. The tradition is unusually blunt about this: the chart tells you the terrain, the season, and the shape of your own instrument. It does not tell you where to walk.

Read it as a map. Argue with it. Then go.

The method

  • 01Year turns at Li Chun — solar longitude 315° — never at Chinese New Year.
  • 02Month turns at each of the twelve jie, solved to the minute from the sun's apparent longitude.
  • 03Day pillar from the Julian Day Number: an unbroken count, immune to calendar reform.
  • 04Hour stem by the Five Rats rule; month stem by the Five Tigers rule.
  • 05Luck pillars stepped from the month pillar, direction by year polarity and sex, start age from the distance to the adjoining jie at three days to the year.

The astronomy

Solar longitude after Meeus, Astronomical Algorithms, with the planetary and lunar perturbation terms, nutation, and an aberration corrected for the earth's true radius vector. Terrestrial Time is recovered from UT with the Espenak–Meeus ΔT polynomials.

Checked against sxtwl (壽星天文曆) over 73,414 consecutive days, 1900–2100: zero disagreements on year, month or day pillar away from term boundaries. Solar-term instants land a mean of 1.5 minutes from the reference, worst case 6.4.

The build

One HTML file. No framework, no bundler, no network. Every visual is generated at runtime: the field is a raymarched volumetric shader with a hand-written bloom, chromatic aberration and grain chain in WebGL2; the motes are a transform-feedback curl-noise flow field; the wheels, the seal and the phase diagram are drawn from procedure.
